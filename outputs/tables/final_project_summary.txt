================================================================================
CORE SCIENTIFIC CONCLUSIONS
================================================================================
1. Short-horizon ARI forecasting was most accurate.
2. The 1-week Negative Binomial ARI-only model achieved the strongest final held-out performance.
3. The 2-week selected model was an ARI-only XGBoost model.
4. The 4-week validation-selected model used an ARI + COVID feature set.
5. However, post-hoc SHAP interpretation indicated that the included COVID predictors contributed little or no importance to the final 4-week predictions.
6. Longer forecast horizons showed reduced predictive skill.
7. COVID censoring assumptions did not materially change the frozen-model results.

================================================================================
INTERPRETATION CAUTIONS
================================================================================
- Validation-selected feature sets should not automatically be interpreted as evidence that every included predictor contributed materially.
- SHAP and gain importance describe predictive model behaviour, not causal effects.
- Held-out 2026 performance must not be used for further model tuning or model re-selection.
- Forecast skill decreased at longer horizons, and MASE exceeded 1 for the 2- and 4-week horizons.
